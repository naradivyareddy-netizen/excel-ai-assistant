 WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------------
A complete, professional Excel troubleshooting and productivity
assistant. Upload any Excel/CSV file and the app instantly finds
errors, explains them in plain English, and fixes them with one
click - then helps you build formulas, charts, macros and reports.


------------------------------------------------------------------
 KEY FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------------
 [+] FORMULA ERROR FIXER
     Detects #VALUE!, #DIV/0!, #N/A, #REF!, #NAME?, #NUM!, #NULL!,
     circular & broken references. Shows the cell, the reason, and
     the fix - then AUTO-FIXES and saves a corrected file.

 [+] DATA CLEANER
     Remove duplicates & blank rows, trim spaces, fix dates,
     standardise text and headers - one click.

 [+] AI FORMULA GENERATOR
     Type plain English ("calculate tax", "top 10 sales") -> get
     the exact Excel formula + explanation.

 [+] PIVOT TABLE ASSISTANT
     Auto-build summary tables grouped by any column.

 [+] DASHBOARD BUILDER
     Instant bar, pie, trend charts and KPI cards from your data.

 [+] VBA / MACRO GENERATOR
     Describe a task -> get ready-to-paste VBA code.

 [+] AI CHAT ASSISTANT
     Ask any Excel question (VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, pivots, Power Query).

 [+] HEALTH CHECK & REPORTS
     Score your workbook's quality and export an audit report.
